# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all participants in the Fluorescence Alert Documentation project. We value the participation of every individual and want to ensure that every person has a positive experience.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful** — Treat all contributors with courtesy and respect
- **Being inclusive** — Welcome people of all backgrounds and experiences
- **Being constructive** — Provide helpful feedback and suggestions
- **Being honest** — Share information and insights truthfully and transparently
- **Being collaborative** — Work together toward common goals
- **Being professional** — Maintain professional communication at all times

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or abuse of any kind
- Insulting or derogatory comments
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Inappropriate sexual comments or advances
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Those who violate the Code of Conduct may be:

- Asked to cease the behavior
- Temporarily suspended from participation
- Permanently banned from the project

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

1. **Document the incident** — Note specific details, dates, and times
2. **Report it** — Contact project maintainers through the repository
3. **Provide context** — Include relevant information to help us understand the situation
4. **Keep it confidential** — We will handle reports professionally and discreetly

All reports will be taken seriously and handled confidentially.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Project-related communications
- Community events and meetings
- Any spaces where the project is represented

## Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:
- Warning or reminder
- Temporary suspension of participation
- Permanent removal from the project
- Other actions deemed appropriate by maintainers

## Our Commitment to Fairness

We are committed to:
- Treating all members fairly and equally
- Considering context and intent
- Providing opportunities for resolution
- Taking violations seriously

## Contact

For Code of Conduct violations or concerns:
- Open an issue in the repository
- Contact project maintainers directly
- Report to appropriate authorities if necessary
